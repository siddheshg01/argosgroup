FINOP Financial Policy Knowledge Base
=====================================

This package contains eight ORIGINAL fictional internal policies created for the FINOP
RAG demonstration. They are designed to be realistic enough for retrieval, citation,
policy matching, and Gemini grounding tests.

Files:
1. revenue_policy.pdf
2. expense_policy.pdf
3. refund_policy.pdf
4. financial_controls.pdf
5. risk_management.pdf
6. fraud_controls.pdf
7. accounting_guidelines.pdf
8. compliance_guidelines.pdf

Important:
- These documents are fictional and are NOT actual company policies.
- They are NOT legal, accounting, tax, regulatory, or compliance advice.
- They are NOT reproductions of COSO, IFRS, RBI, SEC, or other copyrighted standards.
- They are informed by common finance/internal-control concepts and should be replaced or
  supplemented with authoritative, jurisdiction-specific requirements for real deployment.

Suggested RAG test questions:
- What approval is required for a discount above 20%?
- What controls apply to a refund above INR 100,000?
- What evidence is required for an employee expense?
- What are the indicators of potential refund fraud?
- How should a material revenue adjustment be escalated?
- What does the policy say about AI-generated financial decisions?
- What controls apply to month-end close?
- What should happen when no relevant policy document is found?
